How I work

Modern tools. Human judgment.

I use AI extensively—as a research partner, prototyping accelerator, testing aid, and creative production tool. The product decisions, architecture, gameplay, security, and final standard remain mine.

01

Find the real problem

Start with the user, the environment, and the constraint—not the most fashionable implementation.

02

Prototype the risky part

Use code, AI, design tools, and playtests to answer hard questions before polishing the wrong idea.

03

Build the whole experience

Connect systems, interface, content, accessibility, operations, and edge cases into something coherent.

04

Ship, observe, improve

Validate with real use, tighten what matters, document the work, and keep the product maintainable.

About

Creative range, production discipline.

I’m Fredric Shernoff, a developer and designer who enjoys moving between playful systems and serious products. I’ve shipped native mobile apps, classroom platforms, web games, and a commercial PC title.

Earlier work includes MyFandom, a cross-platform live-event fandom product for iOS and Android, and Draw Anything AI, a native iOS creative app. Both products have since been retired, but they remain an important part of my experience shipping consumer software.

I’m especially drawn to work where technology can make learning, community, culture, or storytelling more engaging.
